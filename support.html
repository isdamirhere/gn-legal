Help & contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to gnappinfo@gmail.com. We usually answer within two days.

How do I add friends?

Share your “Add me on GN” link, or search for a friend's @nickname.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile and tap Block or Report. Blocking ends the friendship, and you stop seeing each other.

Can I hide from search?

Yes. Turn off being found by your nickname in Settings. Friends can still add you with your link.

How do I delete my account?

In GN, go to Settings → Delete account. Everything is removed for good. If you can't open the app, write to us from the email you signed up with.
